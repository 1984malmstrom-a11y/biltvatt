These three optimized WebP files are captures of the actual React/CSS Arena
implementation at `/arena/showtime`, not illustrations or proposed interfaces.
They contain only the isolated Showtime synthetic cast and ledger.

Captured from `.showtime-product` at 1920×1080, with the presenter toolbar
outside the captured element, then proportionally reduced to 1440px and
compressed to WebP. Source build: local Night Build, based on checkpoint de24968.

- product-station.webp: Station Mode, seven activities, leader/feed/pulse.
- product-leader.webp: existing Ny etta hero after Peter overtakes Alva.
- product-battle.webp: active two-station Battle Mode with real Fight widget.

Every display in Discover is captioned as a genuine product capture with
simulated results. No customer/employee data, credentials or fake app UI.
